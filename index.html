Who am I?

I'm a motivated IT professional based in Ambala Cantt, Haryana, India. Beyond the degree, I hold ITI trade certifications in Computer Operator & Programming Assistant — the practical, workshop-level training that first connected me with machines, software and people.

Since then my work has covered IT supervision at a defense school, warehouse operations at a life-science company, office administration at a veterans' rest home, and high-volume data operations at Reliance. Each role sharpened a different muscle — but all of them taught me the same thing: systems only work when the people using them are supported.

On the side, I build. My flagship is an offline billing system for hotels, designed in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript to keep working even when the internet doesn't.
